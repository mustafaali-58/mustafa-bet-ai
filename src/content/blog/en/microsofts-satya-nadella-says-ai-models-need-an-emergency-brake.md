# The Need for an 'Emergency Brake' in AI Models

In a recent post, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella highlighted the urgent need for implementing emergency brakes within AI models. As artificial intelligence rapidly transforms industries, it brings along significant concerns regarding safety and ethics. Nadella argues for a critical assessment of the trust architecture of AI, proposing that without these emergency mechanisms, unforeseen risks could arise. Such features would empower AI systems to intervene in adverse situations, thereby enhancing their safety through human oversight. This proactive approach could lead to greater user trust in AI technologies and facilitate the broader adoption of these powerful tools.
