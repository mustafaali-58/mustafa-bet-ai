# Mazama Energy Raises $135M to Drill Deeper into Super-Hot Rock Geothermal

Mazama Energy, backed by Khosla Ventures, is carving its niche in the renewable energy landscape with its ambitious plan to drill three miles underground to tap into super-hot rock geothermal energy. This remarkable technique carries the promise of significantly reducing dependence on traditional energy sources.

The recently secured investment of $135 million will facilitate the company’s initial drilling efforts, with one well projected to generate 15 MW of electricity around the clock. Such innovations not only enhance energy security but also advocate for environmental sustainability in a time when the world increasingly prioritizes renewable energy solutions.

Mazama’s strategy to leverage high-temperature rock benefits highlights a growing potential to drive cleaner and more sustainable energy production, positioning the company as a key player in reshaping the energy industry's future.
