# Harvard's $699 Startup Bootcamp with AI Avatars

Cutting-edge applications of artificial intelligence continue to revolutionize the education sector. Harvard Business School's newly launched startup bootcamp program, known as HBS Foundry, provides participants with instruction through AI avatars. This program is designed to assist entrepreneurs in refining their business ideas and practicing their pitches. Throughout the program, AI avatars offer feedback during practice pitches and board meetings, enriching the participants' experience.

Such applications not only provide entrepreneurs with a competitive edge but also showcase how AI can be effectively utilized in educational settings. For a fee of $699, participants gain access to the knowledge and expertise of Harvard's top instructors while experiencing a tech-enhanced learning environment.

The use of AI technologies in education not only improves individual learning processes but also enables reaching broader audiences. These innovative approaches offer important insights into how educational systems may evolve in the future.
