# HiddenLayer Secures $100M as Enterprises Rush to Safeguard Their AI Deployments

The recent surge in interest towards artificial intelligence (AI) applications has prompted companies to ramp up investments aimed at ensuring the security of these systems. HiddenLayer has successfully raised $100 million in Series B funding from prominent investors including Delta-v Capital, Ten Eleven Ventures, Morgan Stanley, Microsoft's M12, and Booz Allen Hamilton.

This investment marks a significant step forward in the field of AI application security. The company offers protective solutions for businesses deploying AI solutions. The potential vulnerabilities of AI systems, coupled with cybersecurity threats, pose serious risks—especially in critical sectors like fintech. HiddenLayer’s offerings provide a defense mechanism against these threats, enabling businesses to continue innovating without security concerns.

Following this investment, HiddenLayer is poised to expand its customer base and reach companies around the globe. By building the necessary infrastructure for businesses to embrace AI technologies without fear, the company is likely to secure a leading position in future AI and fintech projects.
