# Venture Capital and Sports Ownership: A New Frontier

In recent times, professional sports ownership has emerged as a compelling domain for venture capitalists (VCs). Thrive Capital has taken a significant stride into this new market, laying down an important blueprint for owning sports franchises. Now, Collaborative Fund is following suit by acquiring stakes in D.C. United and its stadium, with founder Craig Shapiro pitching this move as an opportunity to showcase the firm's startups.

Such investments not only promise financial returns but also serve as a platform for brand visibility and innovative business model development within the sports and technology ecosystems. This emerging trend of asset management in sports ownership is likely to lead to significant impacts and transformations in both the sports industry and the tech landscape.
