# Instinct: The Viral AI Assistant Now Has Its Own Email Address

In an exciting development for the realm of artificial intelligence, Instinct, a viral AI assistant, has rolled out a new email feature that allows it to create and manage email accounts, contact businesses, and handle support requests on behalf of its users. This innovation signifies a pivotal moment in how AI can streamline user experiences and automate various tasks.

With this new feature, Instinct can significantly enhance the efficiency of user interactions. For instance, if a user requires assistance with a product, Instinct can directly forward the support request to the relevant business. This kind of integration not only simplifies the user experience but also represents a step toward greater autonomy in AI operations.

The implications of such developments are far-reaching, suggesting that AI assistants will soon be capable of performing tasks with even less human intervention. As Instinct sets the stage for a new era in user-AI interaction, it may also inspire other applications within the Fintech space to broaden their functionalities and continue evolving in a rapidly changing technological landscape.
