# Uber and Pony.ai Plan to Bring 2,000 Robotaxis to Europe

In a revolutionary step for the robotaxi sector, Uber and Pony.ai have announced their plans to roll out 2,000 robotaxis across Europe. Initially setting their sights on Zagreb, Croatia, this partnership is set to expand into four additional European cities. This move is aimed at increasing automation in urban transportation, enhancing user experience, and improving accessibility. 

For years, the technology sector has been working toward the development of autonomous vehicles, and now we are witnessing the fruition of these ambitious efforts. The synergy of advanced engineering and artificial intelligence algorithms is expected to ensure that these robotaxis can operate safely, efficiently, and user-friendly. 

This initiative by Uber and Pony.ai not only marks a significant milestone in the European market but could also stimulate competition, encouraging other tech companies to invest in similar ventures. Additionally, it signifies a critical step towards reducing traffic congestion in cities and lowering greenhouse gas emissions.
