# PNOĒ’s New Face Mask Aims to Make Lab-Grade Breath Testing Self-Serve

Malden, Mass.-based startup PNOĒ is set to revolutionize personal health monitoring with its newly designed breath-analyzing face mask that is both sleek and user-friendly. Launching on October 1, this innovative device allows gym-goers to measure their VO₂ max and other vital metabolic markers in a swift eight minutes — all without the need for a trained operator.

This advancement reflects a significant leap for athletes and health-conscious individuals alike. As lifestyle changes and an increasing desire for personal health insights shape consumer preferences, such an accessible measurement tool becomes invaluable. Breath analysis not only offers solid insights into metabolism but also enhances the development of tailored training and nutrition strategies.

In conclusion, PNOĒ’s latest face mask represents an exciting evolution at the intersection of health and sports technology. As demand for self-serve health solutions continues to rise, the spotlight will undoubtedly shine brighter on innovative products like this, paving the way for a future where personal health management is easier and more informed.
