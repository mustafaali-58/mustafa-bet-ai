# Open or Closed AI: A Decision Shaping Next-Gen Startups

At TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, Nvidia's Nader Khalil and Sydney Sykes tackled one of the most pressing decisions in the AI domain: the choice between open and closed AI implementations. This decision has significant implications for the startup ecosystem, influencing both current operations and future innovations.

Open AI typically allows for rapid advancements through community and developer contributions, fostering an environment rich in innovation. Conversely, closed AI models, while often more controlled, provide targeted and reliable outputs under a single company's governance. The choice between these two models fundamentally impacts a wide range of issues, from ethics and accessibility to business opportunities.

Khalil and Sykes offered insights on which model startups might adopt and how AI will integrate into the business landscape moving forward, prompting attendees to consider new business solutions. As the future of AI continues to evolve around such pivotal choices, the role of businesses in this transformation remains a critical point of discussion.

Ultimately, the preference for either open or closed AI is a matter that warrants careful consideration, not only from a technical standpoint but also from strategic and ethical perspectives.
