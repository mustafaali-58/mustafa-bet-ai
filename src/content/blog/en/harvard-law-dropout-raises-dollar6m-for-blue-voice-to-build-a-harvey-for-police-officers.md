# Harvard Law Dropout Raises $6M for Blue Voice to Build a 'Harvey for Police Officers'

Recently, a Harvard Law dropout has successfully secured significant funding to develop an innovative app aimed at police officers. Known as 'Blue Voice', this application seeks to assist officers by providing real-time legal and policy guidance, ultimately enhancing their decision-making processes. The initiative garnered $6 million in seed funding, led by prominent investors such as SignalFire and Las Olas VC.

The application aims to simplify and make accessible the complexities of the legal system through education and technology. The developed system, named 'Harvey', is designed to help officers navigate various legal scenarios they may encounter, thereby enhancing public safety and enabling officers to perform their duties effectively.

Moreover, this initiative could redefine the intricate relationship between law enforcement and public safety. The interest from investors indicates the potential of this application, and the funding of such technological solutions could pave the way for more innovations in the future.
