The tech world has seen a revolution under the dominance of smartphones. However, the era of smartphones appears to be approaching a conclusion. At the TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 event, Amazon's tech executive Panos Panay will discuss the innovations that will follow this revolution.

Tech enthusiasts will closely follow Panay’s proposed vision, as events like these are often precursors to innovative solutions that will soon permeate our daily lives.
