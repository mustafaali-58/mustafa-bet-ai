# AI, Athletes, and Keith Rabois: StrictlyVC Returns to New York

On September 10th, the influential StrictlyVC evening is making its return to New York's West Village, bringing together key figures like Keith Rabois, Craig Shapiro, Jason Levien, and others to explore pivotal topics in AI, sports investing, community building, venture economics, and politics. As artificial intelligence reshapes investment landscapes, discussions will spotlight the impact of digital transformation in sports and the vital role of data analytics. The event promises ample networking opportunities complemented by cocktails and food, enabling industry insiders to exchange innovative ideas and strategies. Events like this foster collaboration among participants, spurring advancements that could redefine the future of both AI and sporting industries.
