# Nvidia's Jensen Huang: We Don't Need AI Regulation

Nvidia's CEO, Jensen Huang, asserts that AI is not some new form of 'alien mind'; it is merely hardware and software. He confidently stated, "Each AI product maker can engineer safety into their products," expressing skepticism about the necessity for regulatory frameworks surrounding artificial intelligence.  
Huang's perspective shifts the focus back to the industry and emphasizes the capability of engineers and product developers to create secure and reliable AI systems.  
  
In an era where artificial intelligence is advancing rapidly, Huang's remarks spark crucial conversations about the responsibility of individual companies in ensuring ethical and safe AI. As many organizations still grapple with the maturation of AI technologies, this call for self-regulation could lead to varying degrees of compliance and safety measures across the industry, raising important questions about accountability and standardization.
