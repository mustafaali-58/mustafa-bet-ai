# Petlibro's New AI-Powered Feeder is a Game Changer for Multi-Cat Homes

Petlibro has introduced the new Granary 2 smart feeder, addressing a significant concern for multi-cat households. This device employs a built-in scale to track exactly how much your cat is eating and when they are eating. Higher-end models are equipped with an AI camera that offers health monitoring features, although these may require an additional subscription fee.

The Granary 2 empowers users to gain a better understanding of their cats' eating habits. This AI-driven smart feeder not only monitors the amount of food consumed but also provides valuable insights into your pet's health, helping to formulate customized feeding plans tailored to each cat's needs. By enabling data-driven decisions, this technology fosters healthier lifestyles for our feline companions.

As Petlibro’s innovation marks a revolutionary step in the pet tech industry, the integration of AI and IoT technologies exemplifies the future of smart home devices, offering a glimpse into how technology can enhance pet care.
