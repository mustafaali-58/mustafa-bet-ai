# Creating My Own Digital Avatar: The Boundaries of the Future

Recently, I had the opportunity to observe the experience of building and training a digital avatar of myself. This avatar was trained to discuss complex topics, such as venture fraud. However, this process left me with mixed feelings. Digital avatars could be powerful tools that may alter how we express ourselves, but they also encompass a series of ethical dilemmas.

Firstly, the ability of a digital avatar to function like an AI-supported brain holds potential for creating a new form of our personal brand. When people interact with these avatars, they can represent the image we construct of ourselves. Nevertheless, two critical questions arise: To what extent does this avatar reflect our true selves, and how may it influence societal norms?

In particular contexts such as financial fraud, it is essential to raise greater awareness of how digital avatars and AI can be used effectively. In the future, while these technologies could further deepen human interaction, they may also become prone to deception and misinformation.

Listening to the experiences of those who have created digital avatars is crucial for gaining further insights into the challenges and opportunities encountered on this path. Discussing what ethical frameworks should be adopted in both individual and societal contexts will be a critical step in understanding the potential dangers outside this choice. Ultimately, while our personal digital avatars become meaningful surfaces, we must weigh the responsibilities and possibilities of this technology.
