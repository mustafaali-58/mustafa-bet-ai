# Discovered Materials' AI Strategy in Hunting Cooler Chips

Discovered Materials has raised $9 million to pursue the creation of more efficient chips. The company is employing an AI-driven approach that resembles a game of 'whack-a-mole' to tackle the myriad challenges of material discovery. This method focuses on continually adapting and evolving solutions as new obstacles emerge with every discovery.

Artificial intelligence has emerged as one of the most significant game changers in materials science and engineering. Companies like Discovered Materials leverage AI algorithms to find new materials for cooler and more efficient chips. In this process, the rapid analysis and predictive capabilities offered by AI play a critical role in identifying novel components and optimizing intricate craftsmanship.

However, such discovery processes necessitate not only the use of AI but also a fusion of innovative ideas. Success hinges not just on identifying new materials but also on rethinking existing technologies to enhance their efficiency and durability. The $9 million funding for Discovered Materials is a vital step toward realizing these innovative projects and goals.
