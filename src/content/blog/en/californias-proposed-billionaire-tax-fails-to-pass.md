California voters rejected Proposition 40, the proposed one-time 5% tax on billionaires, according to an AP report. Tech leaders outspent backers roughly 10 to 1 to defeat the measure. Founders and economists interviewed by TechCrunch were divided on whether the tax would have driven wealthy residents out or merely reduced their fortunes.
