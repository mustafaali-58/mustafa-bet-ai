# Roku's New Labs Hub Enhances User Experience

Roku is taking user experience to the next level with its latest OS update by introducing Roku Labs, a new hub for experimental apps. Roku Labs aims to increase app diversity on the platform by providing developers with a space to showcase innovative and new solutions.

This new feature makes experimental apps more accessible to users, allowing them to explore applications that are still in the testing phase. Users will have the opportunity to provide feedback on their experiences, offering developers valuable insights to create products that better meet user needs.

Additionally, the update includes personalized home screens and subscription bundles that will be available in more markets, enabling users to discover content in a more organized and personalized manner.

In conclusion, Roku Labs presents a significant opportunity for both users and developers. With this new hub, Roku’s platform is poised to become richer and more dynamic. This technological leap not only enhances the user experience but also represents an important step for the future of experimental applications.
