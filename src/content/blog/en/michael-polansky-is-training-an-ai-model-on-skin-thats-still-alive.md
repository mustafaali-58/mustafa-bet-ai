# AI Model Training on Living Skin: Michael Polansky's Innovative Approach

Michael Polansky, better known publicly as Lady Gaga's partner and a former top deputy to Sean Parker, has spent years quietly working on an AI-driven startup that sustains living human skin tissue for weeks outside the body. This unique approach opens doors to the discovery of new skincare compounds and offers promising avenues for dermatological studies and product development.

The ability to maintain living skin in a lab environment is particularly striking. This innovation allows for the testing of various potential skincare ingredients that could significantly benefit the beauty industry. Historically, utilizing living tissues has been a complex and challenging task; however, Polansky aims to make this process more accessible and efficient.

Looking ahead, the products developed from Polansky's research could transform our skincare routines. Additionally, the AI algorithms driving this technology may have significant applications not only in dermatological assessments but also in broader health research. Ultimately, training an AI model on living skin holds the potential to revolutionize not just skincare but also medical research as a whole.
