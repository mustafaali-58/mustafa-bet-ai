# New Developments in Meta's AI Agent Muse

During the kickoff of the annual Connect event in Menlo Park, CEO Mark Zuckerberg made a significant announcement: Meta is going all-in on Muse. As of 2026, Muse will be integrated into Meta's AI glasses, marking a pivotal advancement in user experience and everyday applications of artificial intelligence. This technology holds the potential to transform not only social media interactions but various aspects of daily life as well. In this post, we will explore the innovations offered by Muse and their implications for the future of this technology.
