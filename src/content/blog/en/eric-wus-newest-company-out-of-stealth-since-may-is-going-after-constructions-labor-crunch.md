# Eric Wu's New Company Tackles Labor Crunch in Construction

Eric Wu, after stepping down from Opendoor in 2022, has emerged with his new venture, NavigateAI, addressing a critical labor shortage in the construction industry. The company has been out of stealth since May and is focused on providing construction workers with AI copilots that offer real-time, hands-free guidance through smartphones and Meta's AI glasses. Backed by $25 million from Elad Gil, Khosla Ventures, and Lennar, NavigateAI is strategically positioned to combat the staggering number of workers required for data center projects, which range from 4,000 to 5,000 workers per project. By effectively leveraging AI technologies, NavigateAI aims to enhance workforce management in construction, fostering efficiency and innovation in the sector.
