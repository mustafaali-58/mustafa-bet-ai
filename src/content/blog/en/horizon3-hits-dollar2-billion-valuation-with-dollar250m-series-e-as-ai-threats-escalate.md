# Horizon3 Hits $2 Billion Valuation with $250M Series E as AI Threats Escalate

Horizon3 has made a significant leap in the cybersecurity landscape by raising $250 million, resulting in a valuation of $2 billion. This remarkable growth stems from the increasing demand for continuous, AI-powered security validation, contrasting the traditional annual pentesting approaches.

As AI threats and cyber attacks become more sophisticated, organizations are compelled to seek consistent and proactive security solutions. Horizon3 addresses this gap by providing an innovative approach that helps CTOs continuously update their security protocols, allowing them to adopt a more dynamic security strategy rather than relying solely on annual audits.

Investors backing this funding round forecast that the integration of AI technology will play a central role in the future of cybersecurity. With this perspective, Horizon3 not only represents a pivotal shift in the cybersecurity domain but also offers significant opportunities for investors. In the coming years, the adoption of such AI-driven solutions is expected to grow, with Horizon3 leading the charge.
